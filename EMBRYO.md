# Embryo Stress

Embryo Stress is Embryo-Zero's fork of [CalculiX](https://github.com/Dhondtguido/CalculiX) (57a4e71, commit 57a4e71). It does strength and heat of parts (finite elements).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
